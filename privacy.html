Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 · Applies to PCVR Nexus for Android and PCVR Nexus Host for Windows

PCVR Nexus streams your PC to your phone over your own Wi‑Fi or USB cable. It was built to work without accounts or servers, so there is very little to say here, and we have tried to say it plainly.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your screen, sound and motion go straight between your PC and your phone. They never pass through us.
  • Stream logs stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.
  • You can forget paired devices or uninstall the apps at any time, and everything they stored goes with them.

Contents

Who we are

PCVR Nexus (the Android app) and PCVR Nexus Host (the Windows app) are made by [developer or company name]. In this policy, “we” means the developer and “the apps” means both programs.

What the apps handle

To stream, the apps need to move some information between your two devices. Here is all of it.

Screen and sound
The PC sends its picture and audio to the phone while a stream is running. It is shown live and is not recorded or saved by either app.
Motion and controls
The phone sends its motion sensor readings (and taps you make on the stream controls) to the PC, so the game camera can follow your head.
Pairing details
The phone’s model name and a random ID created by the app, and your PC’s name. After you pair with the 4‑digit code, each side keeps a pairing key so it can reconnect without the code.
Settings
Your choices, such as stream quality, theme and connection type. Each app keeps its own on its own device.
Stream logs (Android)
Recorded only while a stream is running: phone model and Android version, network type, local network addresses, stream settings, stream health every few seconds, errors and crashes. The newest 15 streams are kept, on the phone only.

What we don’t collect

We do not run any servers for the apps, so nothing is sent to us. The apps don’t ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. There is no advertising, no analytics and no third‑party tracking code in either app.

When anything leaves your devices

Only in these cases, both of which you start yourself:

  • The stream itself travels over your local network or USB cable between your phone and your PC.
  • Sharing logs. If you share stream logs (as a JSON file, through the share menu or WhatsApp), they go to whoever and whatever app you pick. That app’s own privacy policy then applies to the file.

Android permissions

Android asks you to allow some of these. Each one is used only for what is listed here.

Nearby Wi‑Fi devices, local network
To find PCVR Nexus Host on your network and connect to it.
Wi‑Fi and network state
To tell whether you are on Wi‑Fi or USB tethering and pick the right connection.
Notifications
To show the small “streaming” notification that keeps the connection alive in the background.
Motion sensors at a high rate
Smooth head tracking in VR.
Location
Listed only because older Android versions require it for some Wi‑Fi features. The app never asks for it and never reads your location.

Security

A phone can only connect after you type the 4‑digit code shown on your PC. After that, the two devices use a pairing key stored privately inside each app. You can remove that trust at any time: in the Android app under Settings › Paired PCs, or in PCVR Nexus Host with “Forget paired phones”.

Because the stream travels over your own network, use a network you trust. We recommend not streaming over public Wi‑Fi.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Stream logs: only the newest 15 streams are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Paired devices: forget them in either app as described above.
  • Everything else: uninstalling the Android app removes all of its data. On Windows, uninstall PCVR Nexus Host and delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\GameStreamServer.

Children

The apps are not aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the apps start handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change ships.
