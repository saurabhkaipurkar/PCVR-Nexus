Health and safety

Using a phone in a VR headset can cause motion sickness, eye strain, headaches or disorientation. Some people are more sensitive than others.

  • Use VR seated or in a clear space, away from stairs, furniture, pets and other people.
  • Take a break every 20–30 minutes, and stop straight away if you feel dizzy, sick or uncomfortable.
  • Don’t use VR while walking, driving or doing anything that needs your attention.
  • Phones get warm during long streams. Stop if your phone becomes hot, and don’t cover it while charging.
  • VR headsets are not recommended for children under 13.
